## Use
Generated levels use `ABalloonLevelGenerator::SpawnLevelEnemies` in `BalloonLevel/BalloonLevelEnemies.cpp` after successful realization. `Balloon Level | Enemies` exposes the master/shared/biome switches and prototype counts/distances. These take effect on the next generation. Shared spawning reuses an authored airship, and skips native AA when a configured border class supplies the edge threats. Field adds `ANemesisBirdFlock`; Desert adds grounded `ANemesisTowerCannon` and `ANemesisBeacon`. Placements use the generated seed and route, with a start clearance. Actors are transient and tracked by the realizer, so successful regeneration and Clear remove them; failed regeneration preserves them. These switches do not affect manually placed actors, and border influence proxies remain controlled by border settings. Native functional enemies are separate from the existing optional catalog placement budget. `GunsAndBalloons.BalloonLevel.Enemies` covers selection, seeded regeneration, disable switches and cleanup.

Drop `ANemesisAirship` into a level with a balloon (any actor with `UBalloonFlightComponent`). At BeginPlay it jumps `StartDistance` (80 m, nose ≈ 60 m from the basket: D1) behind the balloon (0 = stays where placed). Nothing else is required. Rockets and planes come from behind: look back to see them.

**Enemy test level:** `/Game/Scenes/Enemies_Test` (built by `Scripts/Editor/create_enemies_test_level.py` from BalloonFlight_Test): the balloon, the player with the six hand items in front of them, a 3 × 3 km desert ground 50 m below, and an `ANemesisWaveDirector`. The director spawns the airship 250 m behind (`AirshipStartDistance`; `ANemesisAirship::SetStartDistance` before `FinishSpawning`, otherwise it jumps to its own 80 m = the rocket band) with `bInvulnerableBalloon` on for testing, then runs `Events` (seconds after start, 6–14 s apart; each shows its label on screen): 2 s take a weapon, 6 s bird flock 100 m ahead, 14 s airship crop dusters (`SetForcedBand`), 24 s beacon 60 m ahead / 35 m left on the ground, 36 s airship bomber + mines, 42 s cannon tower 70 m ahead / 45 m right, 56 s airship rockets, 62 s AA balloon 35 m ahead / 18 m left, 76 s crop duster patrol 110 m ahead, 90 s airship back to attacks by distance. Enemies face the balloon; towers and beacons stand on the ground under their point (or `FallbackGroundDepth` 50 m below the basket). Edit `Events` / `bLoop` on the WaveDirector in the level.

**Test level:** `BalloonFlight_Test` has the six hand items (revolver, hook, rifle, shotgun, extinguisher, wrench) in front of the player, attached to `BP_Balloon` so they fly along, since the player now starts with an empty hand. Re-place them with `Scripts/Editor/place_item_pickups_balloon_test.py` (same layout as `place_item_pickups.py` for Movement_Scene1).

## ANemesisAirship
- **Movement.** Every `TrailSampleInterval` (0.5 s) it records the balloon location + `HeightOffset`; it walks that polyline at constant speed = balloon `GetMinSpeed()` (or `SpeedOverride` if > 0, for tests). Yaw eases toward the movement direction.
- **Distance** = nose (`DistanceReference`, local) to the basket (`BasketOffset` on the balloon). Bands: `< KillDistance` Turrets, `< RocketBandMax` Rockets, `< BomberBandMax` Bombers, else CropDusters. Read with `GetDistanceToBalloon()` / `GetBand()`.
- **Turrets.** Inside `TurretWarnDistance` each turret swings its +X to the basket, keeping its mount's up axis; `OnTurretWarning(bool)` fires on change (BP hook for horn/sound). Inside `KillDistance`: `OnTurretsFired()` and `UEngineHealthComponent::Kill()` once.
- **Rockets.** In the Rockets band, one launch every `RocketCooldown` (10 s; was 7 s) while fewer than `MaxRockets` are alive, from `RocketMuzzle` (front of the launcher under the control car). `OnRocketLaunched(Rocket)` is a BP hook.
- **Slowdown.** `ApplyRocketSlowdown()` sets flight modifier `NemesisRocketSlowdown` (speed × `1 − SlowdownPercent`) and clears it after `SlowdownDuration`; a new hit restarts the timer. The flight component clamps the result to `MinSpeed`.
- **Planes.** Bombers band: one `ANemesisBomber` per `BomberCooldown` (max `MaxBombers`). Crop dusters band: one `ANemesisCropDuster` per `CropDusterCooldown` (15 s), at most `MaxCropDusters` (2) of the airship's own, and only while `UNemesisTargetSubsystem::CanAddCropDusters()` (fewer than `MaxAttackingCropDusters` 3 attacking on the map: flying, not down, not on a patrol route; team decision 2026-11-15); the free side first (counting every attacking crop duster). Planes spawn at `HangarMouth` XY at their own altitude (bomber: balloon Z + `HeightAboveBalloon`; duster: basket Z) and keep attacking after the band changes. Mines in the air are capped by `MaxMines` (3; 6 was too many in playtest) (`CanSpawnMine` / `RegisterMine`). No new attacks after the turrets kill the player.
- **Debug.** `bDebugDraw` draws the trail and distance/band; `bForceBand` + `ForcedBand` force one attack type regardless of distance (turrets still kill by distance). `bDebugAllAttacks` runs rockets, bomber and crop dusters at once (each on its own cooldown/limit); `bDebugInvulnerableBalloon` sets `UEngineHealthComponent::bInvulnerable` (hits logged, no health loss, no turret kill) for demos.
- **Collision.** All airship meshes are QueryOnly and block only `Visibility`: bullets stop on it, the balloon flight sweep and physics ignore it.
- **Health fallback.** If the balloon has no `UEngineHealthComponent`, the airship adds one at runtime and logs a warning. Add the component to `BP_Balloon` to tune `MaxHealth`.

## ANemesisRocket
- Its smoke trail is the AA missile's `MissileTrail` (90 puffs/s, dense grey plume) since 2026-11-15.
- `Launch(Airship)`: quadratic Bezier P0 = muzzle, P1 = midpoint + `ArcHeight`, P2 = basket + balloon velocity × flight time (3 iterations on the curve length). Arc-length table of 32 samples → constant `Speed`. After P2 it flies straight along the end tangent.
- **Hit:** the segment flown this frame passes within `BasketHitRadius` of the basket centre → `ApplyDamage(Balloon, Damage)` + slowdown. Envelope hits do nothing.
- Hits `WorldStatic` geometry (line trace) → explodes harmlessly (`bStopOnWorldGeometry`). No timer self-destruct; removed after the curve once farther than `DespawnDistance` from the balloon.
- **Shot down:** the `HitSphere` blocks `Visibility`, so the revolver trace hits it; any damage → explode. `OnExploded(bHitBalloon)` is the BP hook for VFX/SFX.
- The body is a placeholder engine cylinder + red point light.

## Planes (placeholder cubes)
- **ANemesisPlane.** `Launch(Airship)` fixes Z at the spawn height. Each tick: desired velocity = balloon velocity + slot error × `SlotGain` (XY, clamped to `MaxSpeed`), reached with `Acceleration` (inertia). Nose turns to the velocity, or the balloon's heading when hovering; it never points more than 60° away from the balloon's heading (planes don't turn around), except while leaving. `Leave()` flies forward and removes after `LeaveTime`.
- **Hit zones.** All cubes block `Visibility`. Planes count hits, not damage (design 2026-11-14): `OnTakePointDamage` on any of `EngineZones` downs the plane at once (any weapon), it falls with gravity (`FallTime`). Any other part is the fuselage: each hit takes 1/N, N = `RevolverFuselageHits` 4, `ShotgunFuselageHits` 3, `RifleFuselageHits` 2, `OtherFuselageHits` 3; mixed weapons add up. The weapon is the causer pawn's `GetActiveGun()` (gun bullets name the shooter as causer). Pellets of one shotgun shot within `ShotgunPelletWindow` (0.15 s) count as one hit. `GetEngineHealth()` / `GetFuselageHealth()` are 1 → 0. `OnPlaneDowned(bEngine)` BP hook.
- **Hit zones seen from behind.** Planes ahead of the balloon show their tail. The nose `Engine` is wider and taller than the fuselage so its rim shows around it, and the bomber has two extra wing engines (`EngineL`/`EngineR`, ±3 m) in `EngineZones`, so a plane ahead can be downed with one engine hit (rifle). Verified 2026-11-14: real rifle bullet, bomber 42 m ahead, wing engine → down.
- **ANemesisBomber.** Flies `HeightAboveBalloon` (12 m) above the balloon; the airship's bomber is raised at launch to clear the balloon's bounds (+ its half height + `ObstacleClearance` + 1 m, ~18 m), so plane avoidance never blocks its way to the slot ahead (2026-11-15: it got stuck beside the balloon and never dropped mines). Fixed slot = balloon + forward × `AheadDistance` (40 m; the AI doc's 150 m was too far for prototype speeds), on the balloon's course; once there it never changes position (design 2026-11-14). Inside `SlotTolerance` it drops an `ANemesisMine` every `DropIntervalMin..Max` s (7–10 s; 5–7 s was too frequent and 10–13 s too rare in playtest) from the left wing, the belly or the right wing (`LaneOffset` 5 m, a different point each time), so mines land left of, on and right of the course. The mine's fall speed is timed so it is at basket height when the balloon arrives (height ÷ (distance ÷ balloon speed)). Never leaves: holds its slot until shot down (`LifeTime` 0; a positive value is for tests only).
- **ANemesisMine.** Falls at the speed the bomber picked, clamped to `MinFallSpeed..MaxFallSpeed` (0.2–1.5 m/s). Removed once the balloon is `PassedDistance` (20 m) past it, or after `LifeTime`. Shot in the mine → explodes, basket within `Radius` (4 m; 8 m was too big) takes flat `Damage` (30). Shot in the parachute → falls at `CutFallSpeed`, harmless blast after `CutExplodeDelay`. Basket within `TriggerDistance` (4 m) → explodes. Red material + light while the basket is inside `Radius`. Removed after `LifeTime`.
- **Plane avoidance** (`ANemesisPlane`, all planes incl. patrols and leaving ones, 2026-11-15; a teammate saw planes overlapping and flying through the balloon). Each plane's size is a circle around all its parts about the actor's origin plus its half height (`BodyRadius`, `BodyHalfHeight`, from the local bounds at launch, so independent of yaw). Obstacles are bounds boxes: the balloon, every airship, tower cannon, beacon and AA gun (colliding parts only, so searchlight beams don't count). A plane that has just left the hangar ignores its own airship until it is out of it. When the plane's altitude overlaps an obstacle (bombers fly above the balloon), it keeps `ObstacleClearance` 4 m from the box: within `ObstacleAvoidDistance` 20 m the part of its course that heads into it becomes a slide around it (same speed, on the side it was heading for), and inside the clearance it is pushed out (`AvoidGain` 1.5 cm/s per cm). Planes at a similar altitude push each other apart to `PlaneClearance` 4 m (stacked exactly: split sideways by id). After the move `KeepClear` is a hard limit: never inside an obstacle box, and two touching planes each move half the overlap. Checked in PIE: 4 crop dusters moved to the far side of the balloon at its height went round it; a full Enemies_Test run (7 planes, ~110 s) had no contact with the balloon (closest 0.8 m to its box), another plane (2 m), the airship, towers, beacon or AA gun. All values TBD.
- **ANemesisCropDuster.** Fixed slot = balloon + forward × 10 m + right × 30 m × side (crop dusters on the same side, oldest first, take slots `SlotSpacing` 15 m apart along the course: the normal one, behind it, ahead of it, further behind...), at basket height + `HeightAboveBasket` (5 m) at launch. The airship gives each new duster the free side; once in its slot it keeps that side and distance until shot down (design 2026-11-14, no side swaps). Fires only in the slot: `OnBurstWarning` 0.5 s before, then 6 bullets 0.12 s apart, cooldown 4 s, aimed straight at the basket with a 3° cone.
- **Tuning note.** The flight prototype speeds are tiny (`MinSpeed` 0.5 m/s, `MaxSpeed` 3 m/s) while the AI doc assumed ~6 m/s; the bomber distance and mine fall speed above were adjusted to that (playtest feedback 2026-11-14).

## Biome enemies (placeholder cubes; place them in the levels)
All use `UNemesisTargetSubsystem` for the balloon, not the airship. Line of sight = trace on `WorldStatic` ignoring the balloon and its riders.
- **ANemesisTowerCannon** (desert; replaced the tower guard on 2026-11-15). **Model:** the team's `SM_TurretTower` (`/Game/Assets/Locations/Desert`, 26 m lattice tower) carries our steampunk cannon and fuel tank (2026-11-15, `Content/Assets/Enemies/TowerGun.blend`, built by `Scripts/Blender/build_tower_gun.py`): `Carriage` = `SM_TowerGun_Mount` (riveted turntable, carriage cheeks, armoured shield, gauge; pivot at its base) on socket `head`, `Barrel` = `SM_TowerGun_Barrel` (one heavy iron cannon with brass bands and copper recoil cylinders; pivot at the trunnion `TowerGunTrunnion` (15, 0, 135) cm, muzzle `TowerGunMuzzle` 430 cm along it), `FuelTank` = `SM_TowerGun_FuelTank` on socket `engine`: a red-painted riveted tank with a yellow/black hazard band on a 1.6 m iron stand (a gravity-fed tank), so it sits above the gun shield at the player's eye level and can be shot from any side (at deck height the shield hid it). They face +X (no model rotation) and use per-poly collision (`setup_enemy_model.py`). Without them the team's set is used: `Carriage` = SM_TurretTower_Head on socket `head` (turns), `Barrel` = SM_TurretTower_Guns on the head's socket `guns` (elevates; shells leave `muzzle_1` / `muzzle_2` in turn), `FuelTank` = SM_TurretTower_Engine (the boiler, weak spot) on socket `engine`, `TankHitBox` sized to it. With `bDeckAtPlayerHeight` (default on, 2026-11-15) the tower is stretched vertically at BeginPlay (`TowerZScale`; the cubes raise `TowerHeight` instead) so the gun deck is at the basket's height + `DeckHeightOffset` (−1.5 m): place it on the ground. The meshes face −Y, so they are turned 90° to the actor's +X; positions come from the sockets in `OnConstruction`, and the explosions run down from the deck (~20 m). Falls back to the cubes below if any part is missing. Pivot at the tower's foot, `TowerHeight` 25 m (solid, the balloon collides with it), a 4 × 4 m platform on top with the cannon (`CannonYaw` / `CannonPitch`, `TurnSpeed` 60°/s) and a red `FuelTank`. Basket within `Range` 180 m and in sight (and no sandstorm: `UNemesisTargetSubsystem::SetSandstormActive`) → the cannon turns to it, `Aiming` for `AimTime` 2 s with an orange glow at the muzzle → one shell (`ANemesisBullet` scaled ×2.5, 250 m/s, `Damage` 12, no drop) with a muzzle blast → `Reloading` 3 s (a shot every 5 s, team damage table 2026-11-15). Losing sight resets the aim. Our fuel tank (2026-11-15, bigger: 2 m across, 3.9 m long) hangs under the deck below the tower's centre (`TowerDeckThickness` 70 cm × `TowerZScale` below the deck top), where nothing on the deck hides it; the tower ignores the Visibility channel so shots reach it through the lattice (it still blocks the balloon and the cannon's WorldStatic line of sight). Hits on the cannon (`Carriage` / `Barrel`) also destroy it: `RevolverCannonHits` 4, `ShotgunCannonHits` 3, `RifleCannonHits` 2, `OtherCannonHits` 3 (one shotgun shot is one hit), then the same `BlowUp`. One hit on the invisible `TankHitBox` (2.2 × 2.2 × 2.6 m around the tank) blows the tank up together with the cannon (2026-11-15): the mount, barrel and tank disappear in two of the AA missile's fine-grained explosions (`NemesisFx::FineExplosion`, scale 3 at the tank and 2 at the cannon; the muzzle blast is a 0.5 one), and the tower stays standing with an empty deck that burns briefly and smokes for `WreckSmokeTime` 8 s (no collapse, by design). `DeckHeight` is set again in `BeginPlay`, because plain members set in `OnConstruction` don't reach the PIE copy of a placed actor (it was 0 in PIE). The cannon and tower ignore shots. Events `OnAimChanged`, `OnShot`, `OnTowerDestroyed`.
- **ANemesisBeacon** (desert). **Model** (2026-11-15): the team's `SK_Lighthouse` (`/Game/Assets/Locations/Desert`, 38 m, lamp bone at 32.6 m) on a `UPoseableMeshComponent` `Body`, turned 90° (it faces −Y) and scaled uniformly so its lamp bone is at the lamp height below (×1.6 in Enemies_Test); `UpdateModelPose` turns its `head` bone with the beam's yaw, keeping the bone scale. Our beam and spot light leave the lamp; the cube `Tower` stays as an invisible 4 m collision column, the cube `Lamp` is hidden and `LampHitBox` (scaled with the model) is the target. A broken lamp gets `BrokenLampMaterial` on the glow slot and its `lamp` bone is tilted and shrunk. Cubes if the model is missing. Lamp on a tower (`TowerHeight` 15 m; with `bLampAtPlayerHeight`, default on since 2026-11-15, the tower grows at BeginPlay so the lamp is at the basket's height + `LampHeightOffset`, so the player can shoot it — place it on the ground) sweeps an **orange** visible cone ±`SweepAngle` 60° around the actor's forward at 20°/s, tilted up `SearchPitch` 8°; beam 15°, `Range` 60 m (2026-11-15; was 200 m). Detection treats the beam as a vertical fan (horizontal angle only) + range (never beyond `TrackRange`) + line of sight. **Once the beam touches the basket it locks on**: the light turns **red** and follows the basket in yaw and pitch at `TrackSpeed` 60°/s with no sweep limit, through cover, until the lamp is shot or the basket is farther than `TrackRange` 80 m (TBD; was 250 m) from the beacon; then it swings back into its sweep and searches again. `SpotTime` 1 s after locking on, and then while it stays locked: one crop duster via `ANemesisAirship::RequestCropDuster()` (from the hangar; not counted toward the airship's own `MaxCropDusters`) whenever `CanAddCropDusters()` and its `Cooldown` (7 s) has passed. While the map is full the cooldown is held at 7 s, so the next call comes 7 s after a place frees up (team decision 2026-11-15; was a pair per spotting, 15 s cooldown). `OnBalloonSpotted` fires once per lock. Shooting the lamp before it spots you prevents the call. The lamp is a 1.6 m cube with an invisible `LampHitBox` (2.6 m, `LampHitBoxExtent`) around it, so it can be hit at range. One hit on either breaks only the lamp (2026-11-15): a small `FineExplosion` (flash, sparks, smoke), light and cone off, the lamp turns dark (`BrokenLampMaterial`), shrinks a little and hangs askew, and smokes for `BrokenSmokeTime` 6 s; the tower stays standing. Events `OnLitChanged(bool)` (lock on / lost / lamp broken while locked), `OnBalloonSpotted(bLaunched)`, `OnLampBroken`; `IsLocked()`.
- **ANemesisFlak** (AA gun, any biome; place it near the map edges). **Model:** the team's `SK_RocketBalloon` (`/Game/Assets/Map_edge`, red-and-cream envelope, deck with a red lamp, 6-tube rocket pod) on a `UPoseableMeshComponent` `Body`, turned 90° (it faces −Y) and lowered 2 m so the `launcher` bone sits at the actor's origin (the player's altitude). `UpdateModelPose` turns bone `launcher` (yaw) and `launcher_pitch` (elevation about the mesh's X) in component space from the reference pose, keeping each bone's scale (the root bone carries a ×100 import scale; dropping it collapsed the pod). Missiles leave `muzzle_01`…`muzzle_06` in turn. The searchlight sits on socket `light` (the red lamp, fixed on the deck) and is aimed in world space each tick. The poseable mesh has no collision, so `EnvelopeCollision` (sphere r 3.6 m), `SkirtCollision` (capsule) and `DeckCollision` (box) follow the team's manifest. The cubes below are hidden; they stay as the fallback. Not used: the team's own `BP_RocketBalloon` / `ABP_RocketBalloon` logic. A small **flying balloon**: `Envelope` sphere (5 × 6 m) on four ropes over a `Gondola` cube (the actor's origin); the turret (yaw) hangs under the gondola so it turns all the way round, with the barrel and searchlight on its pitch pivot. It keeps the X/Y where it was placed and hovers at the **player's altitude**: basket height + `AltitudeOffset` 0, snaps there on the first frame, then follows at `ClimbSpeed` 3 m/s with a 40 cm / 4 s bob (`bMatchPlayerAltitude` off = stays where placed). Envelope and gondola are solid. `EngageRadius` is small on purpose — 25 m (TBD; 120 → 50 → 25 m): a row of AA guns along the map edge marks the line the player must not cross, it is not a constant threat. The searching beam is as long as the radius (`BeamLength` 0 = `EngageRadius`), so its circle shows the danger zone. Outside the radius the **orange** light circles the sky (`SearchSpeed` 25°/s at `SearchPitch` 10°; pitch limited to ±60°). Basket within the radius and in sight → **red**, launcher and light track the basket at `TrackSpeed` 90°/s; after `WarmUp` 1 s, once the launcher is within `FireTolerance` 10°, it launches an **`ANemesisHomingMissile`** (team decision 2026-11-15, replaced the bullet bursts). One missile in the air at a time; the next one `MissileCooldown` 5 s after the last is gone. The missile accelerates from 3 to 9 m/s over 1.5 s (was 5 → 18 m/s: from ~40 m it hit in ~2.5 s, too fast to react; now ~4.5–5 s, enough to get out of the radius, still 3× the balloon's 3 m/s top speed so it can't be outrun inside it), turns towards the basket at up to `TurnRate` 90°/s, deals `Damage` 50 on a direct hit (`BasketHitRadius` 1.5 m), blows up harmlessly as soon as its AA gun stops engaging (the basket left the radius), hits level geometry, or after 15 s; one shot from any weapon blows it up (all values TBD). The missile uses the team's `SM_Rocket` (1.6 m) and `SM_FX_RocketFlame` (`/Game/Assets/Map_edge`), turned from −Y to +X and spun around its flight axis; the airship rocket's model is the fallback. Its trail is its own smoke kind, `NemesisFx::EKind::MissileTrail` (2026-11-15): many small, dense grey puffs (90/s, 0.4 → 1.9 m) that overlap into one plume and widen and swirl as they age, near-flat shading (`RimShade` 0.9) and 64 px puff textures so thinning smoke breaks into fine grain instead of a sparse grid of white dots. It explodes with `NemesisFx::FineExplosion` (scale 1.5): a 0.12 s orange light flash, a fireball of ~90 small fine-grained puffs, then a dark swirling smoke cloud (~70 puffs, 2–4 s), instead of the shared cartoony blast. `NemesisFx::Spawn` now spawns smoke deferred, so per-kind texture settings (`TextureSize`, `ShapeVariants`, `RimShade`) apply before `AGunSmoke::BeginPlay` builds the textures. Released beyond radius + `ReleaseMargin` 5 m (30 m) or when sight is lost. Indestructible (decided 2026-11-15): the player can only avoid it. Events `OnEngagedChanged(bool)`, `OnShot`; `IsEngaged()`.
- **Searchlights** (`NemesisSearchlight.h`): the spot light lights what it hits (volumetric fog scattering off: pointed at the camera it flooded the screen); the cone mesh is the visible beam. Searching = orange (1, 0.42, 0.04), cone `Intensity` 0.18 (plain yellow was lost against the bright sky); locked = red, 0.1 — soft at the edges, almost invisible from inside, so the locked player sees a dim red haze and a red-lit basket.
- **ANemesisBirdFlock** (field). **Model** (2026-11-15): each bird is a `USkeletalMeshComponent` with the team's `SK_Bird` (`/Game/Assets/Locations/Field`) looping `A_Bird_Flap` at its own rate (0.85–1.2) and phase, turned 90° (it faces −Y) to fly nose first, scaled to `BirdWingspan` 1.5 m (TBD); a shot bird stops flapping and falls. The model's root bone carries a ×100 import scale that the animation keys at ×1, so the component is scaled by the reference root scale (read at spawn) to make up for it. Cubes if the model is missing. Trigger actor: balloon within `ActivationDistance` 150 m and no other active flock (`MaxActiveFlocks` 1) → 8–15 birds appear `StartSideDistance` 60 m to one side and fly (boids: separation, cohesion, alignment, goal) to where the balloon will be when they arrive, at `FlightHeight` 1.8 m above the basket centre (a little above the player's head; eye ≈ 1.6 m above the floor, birds ≈ 2.4 m), then on to the other side; 8 m/s. A bird within `BirdHitRadius` 2 m of that strike point deals 3 (max 15 per flock) and is gone. One hit kills a bird (falls); a hit or any player shot whose line passes within `ScareRadius` 10 m of the flock **scares it off for good** (2026-11-15): the birds flee away from the shot and the balloon and climb, never strike, and the flock removes itself 250 m away. The flock removes itself when all birds are gone or 250 m away after crossing.
- **ANemesisPatrol** (lava). Closed `Route` spline (default 300 m loop, edit in the level); spawns `GroupSize` 1 plane (team decision 2026-11-15; was 2) of `PlaneClass` (crop duster by default, or bomber) that fly the route at its altitude (`ANemesisPlane::SetRouteTarget`), `Spacing` 25 m apart. A plane within `AggroDistance` 200 m of the basket while the basket is within `DisengageDistance` 400 m of the route → the group attacks by its type's rules (dusters one per side, bombers ±12 m side by side), if a crop duster patrol fits under `CanAddCropDusters(planes)` and fewer than `MaxEngagedPatrols` 2 groups attack. Basket more than 400 m from the route → back to the route. Shot-down planes are not replaced.

## Enemy models (2026-11-14)
Crop duster, bomber, mine and rocket use real meshes; the beacon and the birds use the team's SK_Lighthouse and SK_Bird; the tower cannon stands on the team's Desert SM_TurretTower with our `TowerGun` cannon and fuel tank; the AA gun and its missile use the team's models (Map_edge SK_RocketBalloon and SM_Rocket) until the team's models land. Each part that is a hit zone or moves is its own mesh (`NemesisModel::Use` in `NemesisPlaceholder.h` swaps a cube for it and keeps the cube if the asset is missing):

A steampunk cruise missile (2026-11-15): angular flat-bottomed body with a wedge nose and a dorsal spine, riveted steel plates, brass bands, copper nose cap, copper steam pipes along the sides, a pressure gauge on the spine, small X tail fins; ~2.7 m, ~580 tris. ## Effects (2026-11-14)
All drawn with the teammates' `AGunSmoke` (blocky dithered PS1 puffs with air physics), one smoke actor per effect; `NemesisFx::FStream` emits continuously and spreads puffs along the path since the last frame (unbroken trails at any frame rate); a stopped stream's actor removes itself after its last puffs fade. `AGunSmoke` shows dark tints much lighter than their value, so "black" smoke uses ~0.01.
- **Engine hit:** the plane falls trailing dense black smoke from the engine (32 puffs/s); after `FallTime` it explodes and is removed.
- **Fuselage destroyed:** a small blast, then the plane falls on fire (flame puffs 40/s, flickering orange `FireLight`) trailing black smoke; explodes at the end of the fall. (Before, the plane vanished at once.)
- **Rocket:** ignition puff at the launcher (`FineBlastSmoke`), motor run-up from `LaunchSpeed` 9 m/s to 25 m/s over `BoostTime` 0.7 s (the intercept point accounts for it), roll `SpinSpeed` 540°/s, flickering flame scale and light (the flame mesh's pivot is at the nozzle, so the flicker keeps it attached), the AA missile's `MissileTrail` plume (`TrailRate` 90/s) that hangs in the air, and the AA missile's `NemesisFx::FineExplosion` (scale 1.5) when it hits or is shot down (2026-11-15; was the shared cartoony blast).
- **Mine:** explosion when it goes off (in place or after the parachute is shot).

## UEngineHealthComponent
`ApplyEngineDamage`, `Kill`, `GetHealth`, `GetHealth01`, `IsDead`, delegates `OnHealthChanged(Health, Delta)`, `OnDestroyed`. It listens to the owner's `OnTakeAnyDamage`, so any `ApplyDamage` on the balloon lands here. It remembers the last damage causer; `GetDeathCause()` turns it into the death screen's text (a bullet counts as its owner; `Kill(Causer)` from the turrets passes the airship). On death: flight tick stops and `ABalloonPlayerController::ShowDeath` plays the death sequence and shows `ADeathScreen` / `UDeathScreenWidget` ([ui-menus](../features/ui-menus.md)); only without that controller does `bRestartLevelOnDeath` show a red message and reload after `RestartDelay`.

**Radar** (`UNemesisRadarSubsystem`, 2026-11-15). The radar is the team's: `BP_Balloon` reads every actor with a `BPC_RadarBlip` into `MPC_Radar` (`Enemy0..15`, `RadarRangeM` 300) and `M_Display` draws them; `BP_JammerTower` writes `RadarJam`. This game-world subsystem gives every enemy that component: on world begin play and on every actor spawn, an enemy without one gets a `BPC_RadarBlip` (created from C++, its Blueprint fields `Kind`, `WarnOrangeRadiusM`, `WarnRedRadiusM` set by reflection before it registers). The table (`RadarBlipFor`) covers the ANemesis* enemies and the generator's `BP_TurretTower_C`, `BP_Lighthouse_C`, `BP_BirdFlock_C`, `BP_RocketBalloon_C`. No Blueprint is edited. `UNemesisRadarSubsystem::RemoveFromRadar(Actor)` destroys the actor's blip component (its EndPlay takes it off `BP_Balloon`'s list); it is called from `ANemesisPlane::GoDown`, `ANemesisTowerCannon::BlowUp`, the beacon's broken lamp and `ANemesisBirdFlock::Scatter`, so a falling plane or a standing wreck no longer stays on the radar. Checked in Enemies_Test PIE: the airship (oval), crop dusters, bomber, tower and beacon fill the slots as they appear.
